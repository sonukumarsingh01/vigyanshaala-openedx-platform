## 🏫 About the client

VigyanShaala equips young people, especially girls, with STEM skills through project-based
learning, to enable social and transformational change. They run their learning programmes on Open edX.

## 📌 The business problem

A default Open edX LMS wasn't enough. VigyanShaala needed:
- a **branded public website** to attract and enrol learners
- **live classes** without sending learners to outside tools
- **engagement and access control** that Open edX doesn't offer out of the box

## ✅ My contribution (about 145 pull requests across all their apps)

| Area | What was built | Value for the client |
|---|---|---|
| 🌐 **Public marketing website** (a new app) | Home, about, contact, FAQ, privacy and terms; a course catalogue with categories, sorting and filters; course pages with preview videos, testimonials and highlight ribbons | Attracts and converts learners on their own branded site |
| 🎥 **Live classes inside the LMS** | Zoom sessions (one-time and recurring), join from the course, recordings, and an attendance report download | No separate video tool; attendance is tracked automatically |
| 🏆 **Engagement** | A leaderboard, notifications, "My Learning" in the header, clickable course cards | Learners stay motivated and find their courses faster |
| 🔒 **Access control** | Restriction pages across learning, discussions, gradebook and communications | Content opens only for the right learners |
| 👤 **Sign-up and profile** | SSO, consent and "remember me" on login, custom profile and account pages | A smooth, compliant onboarding |
| 🎨 **Full brand theme** | A custom header, footer and user menu as Tutor plugins; brand styles across every app | Looks like VigyanShaala's own product |

## 📈 Business impact

- A complete **branded learning business on one platform**: marketing site, LMS and live classes.
- **No extra cost** for a separate website builder or video platform.
- **Upgrade-safe:** every customisation lives in plugins and plugin slots, not in forks.

**Tech:** React, Redux, Open edX micro-frontends, Frontend Plugin Framework, Tutor plugins, Zoom integration, REST APIs, SCSS.
